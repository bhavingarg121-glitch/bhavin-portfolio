SchemeSathi

Solving the problem of scheme awareness in India

🚨 The Problem

Government schemes exist, but most people are unaware of them. Information is scattered, complex, and difficult to access.

Where It Started

People missing benefits due to lack of awareness.

The Realization

The real issue was accessibility, not availability.

The Idea

A simple platform to discover schemes easily.

Building

Focused on clean UI and fast search.

The Goal

Make opportunities accessible to everyone.

⚙️ What It Does

Search. Filter. Discover. Simple and fast experience.

📈 The Impact

Helping people access benefits and improving awareness.
