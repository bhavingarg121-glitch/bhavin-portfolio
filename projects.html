SchemeSathi

A story of solving a real-world problem

It Started With a Problem

Many people are unaware of government schemes. Not because they don’t exist… but because they are hard to find.

A Simple Question

“Why isn’t there one simple place to find everything?”

Building the Solution

That idea became SchemeSathi — a platform to simplify access to government schemes.

What It Does

Search. Filter. Discover. All in a fast, clean, and simple interface.

The Impact

Making information accessible. Helping people find what they deserve.
