SchemeSathi

A platform designed to bridge the gap between government schemes and people who are unaware of them.

Project Title & Context

SchemeSathi is a platform built to connect people with government schemes in a simple and accessible way. Many individuals, especially in rural and semi-urban areas, miss out on important benefits simply because they are unaware of them or find the information too complex to understand.

This project was created to solve that problem by making scheme-related information centralized, easy to understand, and accessible to everyone.

Why I Made This

Many people miss out on government benefits simply because they lack awareness.

Even when information exists, it is often scattered across multiple sources and written in complex language.

I wanted to create a platform that removes this confusion and makes schemes simple, clear, and accessible.

How I Got the Idea

The idea for SchemeSathi came from observing real-life situations around me. Many people were not taking advantage of schemes they were eligible for.

The issue was not availability, but lack of awareness and understanding.

SchemeSathi was built to act as a bridge between government and citizens.

What It Does

  • Provides a centralized platform for government schemes
  • Helps users identify schemes based on eligibility
  • Simplifies complex scheme information
  • Saves time by avoiding multiple sources

Problem Statement

Government schemes are scattered across different sources and often written in complex formats, making them difficult to understand and access.

This creates a major gap between the government and the people who need these benefits the most.

Technologies Used

  • HTML – Structure
  • CSS – Styling
  • JavaScript – Interactivity

Another challenge was maintaining a clean layout while handling large amounts of information.

I solved this by structuring content into sections and focusing on readability and spacing.
