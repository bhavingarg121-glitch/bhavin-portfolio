SchemeSathi

A platform designed to bridge the gap between government schemes and people who are unaware of them.

Project Title & Context

SchemeSathi is built to connect people with government schemes in a simple and accessible way. The idea came from observing that many people miss benefits due to lack of awareness or complex information.

Objective / Problem Statement

Government schemes are scattered and difficult to understand, especially for rural users. This creates a gap between the government and the people who need support.

Tools & Technologies Used

  • HTML5
  • CSS3
  • JavaScript

Your Role & Contributions

I designed and developed the entire project, focusing on UI/UX, responsive design, and simplifying complex information into an easy format.

Features & Functionality

  • Centralized scheme information
  • Simple and clean UI
  • Responsive design
  • Easy navigation

Challenges & Solutions

Simplifying complex data was challenging. I solved this by restructuring content and focusing on clarity and readability.

Impact / Results

This project demonstrates how technology can bridge real-world gaps and improve accessibility of information.

Future Improvements

Currently, SchemeSathi is a basic product that helps people access scheme information. With better response and development, it has the potential to become a highly successful and impactful platform.
