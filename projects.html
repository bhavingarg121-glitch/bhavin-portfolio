Scheme Sathi

Bridging the gap between government schemes and people

The Problem

Every year, thousands of people miss opportunities — not because they are not eligible, but because they simply don’t know.

A student misses a scholarship. A farmer misses a subsidy. A worker misses support.

This is not a technology problem. It is an awareness problem.

The Idea

What if all government schemes were available in one place — simple, clear, and easy to understand?

That’s where Scheme Sathi comes in.

The Solution

A platform where users can filter schemes, understand eligibility, and take action without confusion.

Not just information — but clarity and accessibility.

Impact

Because one right piece of information can change someone’s life.
