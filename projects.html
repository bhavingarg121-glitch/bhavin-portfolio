SchemeSathi by Bhavin Garg

A real-world problem solving platform

The Problem

Government schemes exist, but most people are unaware of them. Information is scattered, complex, and difficult to access.

Where It Started

The idea came from observing people missing opportunities due to lack of awareness.

The Realization

The problem was not schemes, but accessibility and clarity.

The Idea

A platform where users can easily discover and understand schemes.

Building

Focused on usability, speed, and real-world impact.

The Goal

Help people access opportunities easily and efficiently.

What It Does

Search. Filter. Discover. Fast and simple experience.

The Impact

Making scheme access simple and improving awareness.
