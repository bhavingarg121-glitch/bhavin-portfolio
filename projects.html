SchemeSathi

A platform designed to bridge the gap between government schemes and people who are unaware of them.

Project Title & Context

SchemeSathi is a platform built to connect people with government schemes in a simple and accessible way. Many individuals, especially in rural and semi-urban areas, miss out on important benefits simply because they are unaware of them or find the information too complex to understand.

This project was created to solve that problem by making scheme-related information centralized, easy to understand, and accessible to everyone.

Why I Made This

Many people miss government benefits due to lack of awareness.

Information is scattered and difficult to understand.

I wanted to create a simple and accessible solution.

How I Got the Idea

I observed that many people around me were eligible for government schemes but never applied.

The main issue was lack of awareness and clarity.

This inspired me to build SchemeSathi.

What It Does

  • Centralized government scheme information
  • Eligibility-based awareness
  • Simple explanations of complex schemes
  • Easy access for all users

Problem Statement

Government schemes are scattered across different sources and often written in complex formats, making them difficult to understand and access.

This creates a major gap between the government and the people who need these benefits the most.

Technologies Used

  • HTML – Structure of the website
  • CSS – Styling, layout, and responsiveness
  • JavaScript – Interactivity and animations

My Role

I independently designed and developed the entire SchemeSathi project.

My responsibilities included UI/UX design, structuring content, building responsive layouts, and ensuring a clean user-friendly experience.

I focused on simplifying complex government scheme data into understandable content.

Features & Functionality

  • Clean and responsive UI
  • Structured scheme information
  • Easy navigation
  • Fast and lightweight design
  • User-friendly interface

Challenges

One of the biggest challenges was simplifying complex government scheme data into easy language.

Another challenge was maintaining clarity while handling large amounts of information.

I solved this by structuring everything into clean sections.

Impact

SchemeSathi demonstrates how technology can solve real-world awareness problems.

It improved my skills in frontend development, UI design, and problem-solving.

It also helped me understand real user needs.

Future Improvements

AI-based scheme recommendations

Multilingual support

Real-time government updates

Smarter user personalization
