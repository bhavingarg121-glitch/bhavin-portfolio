SchemeSathi

A real-world problem solving platform by Bhavin Garg

The Problem

Government schemes exist, but most people are unaware of them. Information is scattered, complex, and difficult to access.

The Idea

What if there was one simple platform where anyone could easily discover all schemes in one place?

The Solution

SchemeSathi simplifies access to schemes using filtering, clean UI, and fast search.

What It Does

Search. Filter. Discover.
Designed for simplicity and speed.

The Impact

Helping people access benefits easily and improving awareness at scale.
