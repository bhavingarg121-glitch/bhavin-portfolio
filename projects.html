SchemeSathi

A platform designed to bridge the gap between government schemes and people who are unaware of them.

Project Title & Context

SchemeSathi is a platform built to connect people with government schemes in a simple and accessible way. Many individuals, especially in rural and semi-urban areas, miss out on important benefits simply because they are unaware of them or find the information too complex to understand.

This project was created to solve that problem by making scheme-related information centralized, easy to understand, and accessible to everyone.

Why I Made This

Many people miss out on government benefits simply because they lack awareness.

Even when information exists, it is often scattered across multiple sources and written in complex language.

I wanted to create a platform that removes this confusion and makes schemes simple, clear, and accessible.

How I Got the Idea

The idea for SchemeSathi came from observing real-life situations around me. I noticed that many people were not taking advantage of schemes they were eligible for.

The issue was not availability, but lack of awareness and understanding.

That’s when I realized there is a gap between the government and citizens. SchemeSathi was built to act as that bridge.

My goal was to ensure that no one misses out on benefits due to lack of information.

What It Does

  • Provides a centralized platform for government schemes
  • Helps users identify schemes based on eligibility
  • Simplifies complex scheme information
  • Saves time by avoiding multiple sources
  • Improves accessibility of information

How It Helps

SchemeSathi ensures that people do not miss opportunities due to lack of awareness.

It helps users easily discover benefits related to education, finance, and welfare programs.

It empowers individuals by providing clear and structured information, helping them make informed decisions.

My Vision

Currently, SchemeSathi is a basic product that helps people access scheme-related information.

With strong user response and continuous improvement, it has the potential to grow into one of the most impactful and widely used platforms.

My vision is to make it a trusted platform where accessing government benefits becomes simple and fast.

Future plans include AI recommendations, multilingual support, and real-time updates.

Problem Statement

Government schemes are scattered across different sources and often written in complex formats, making them difficult to understand and access.

This creates a major gap between the government and the people who need these benefits the most.

Technologies Used

  • CSS – Styling and responsiveness
  • JavaScript – Interactivity

Features & Functionality

  • Easy navigation

  • User-focused design

Challenges

Another challenge was designing a responsive and clean interface.

These were solved through structured design and user-focused thinking.

Impact

It improved my skills in UI design, problem-solving, and building meaningful applications.

Future Improvements
