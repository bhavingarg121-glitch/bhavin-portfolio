🌟 Why I Built Scheme Sathi

Many people miss out on government benefits because they don’t know about them. Students lose scholarships, workers remain unaware of welfare programs, and farmers miss subsidies — not because they don’t qualify, but because they don’t know. Scheme Sathi acts as a bridge between government schemes and the people who need them most. My goal is to make sure no opportunity is wasted just because of lack of awareness.

🤝 Why Choose Scheme Sathi

  • Trustable & Reliable: All information comes directly from verified government sources.
  • Clear & Simple: Complex schemes are explained in easy language so everyone can understand.
  • Inclusive: Categories for students, workers, farmers, women, and senior citizens ensure relevance for all.
  • Timely Updates: Regularly refreshed so you never miss deadlines or announcements.

Scheme Sathi is not just a website — it’s a trustworthy companion and a bridge between the government and the people.

🎯 My Mission

To empower people by making government schemes accessible, understandable, and timely — ensuring that no opportunity is lost due to lack of information.

🚀 My Vision

To build Scheme Sathi into the most trusted platform for government scheme awareness, connecting millions of citizens with opportunities that can change their lives.
