Computer Science Engineering Student | Samba, J&K

My Journey

Childhood & Kushan Public School

Grew up in Samba, J&K with supportive parents Aman Gupta and Rajni Gupta. Studied at Kushan Public School, where I spent most of my school life. Won Student of the Year (7th class) and represented my district in the SOF Olympiad (5th class).

Discovering Computers

As an introvert, I found my best companion in the computer. I spent hours exploring how apps run, how websites take input, and how layouts are designed. Learned HTML as my first programming language, sparking my passion for technology.

Adarsh Vidya Peeth

In 10th grade, I joined Adarsh Vidya Peeth. The teachers and staff were supportive, focusing on academics and student futures. My last years of school were enjoyable and memorable, strengthening my foundation.

PMSSS Scholarship

Awarded the prestigious PMSSS scholarship, enabling me to pursue engineering outside J&K. Allotted to Priyadarshini College of Engineering, Nagpur, where the beautiful campus and supportive environment motivated me to stay consistent in my studies.

College Achievements

In my first year, I won Second Prize in Chemistry in Action for presenting the Blue Bottle Experiment demonstrating oxidation–reduction reactions.

Current Focus

Currently building a strong foundation in C programming, Python, and Data Structures & Algorithms (DSA). Working on Scheme Sathi, a project using Python, HTML, and CSS to collect and organize information about government schemes, making them more accessible to the public.

Future Goals

Committed to continuous learning, innovation, and impactful projects. Aspiring to grow into a skilled software developer and problem solver who contributes to meaningful solutions and creates value for society.
