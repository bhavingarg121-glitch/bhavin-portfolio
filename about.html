About Me

I am Bhavin Garg, a Computer Science student and aspiring developer focused on solving real-world problems. My journey is driven by curiosity, consistency, and a strong belief that technology should create meaningful impact — not just functionality.

Early Years

I grew up in Samba, J&K, where I developed confidence and curiosity through academics and competitions. Being recognized as Student of the Year and participating in Olympiads shaped my mindset early.

Discovering Technology

As an introvert, I found comfort and curiosity in computers. Understanding how websites and systems work became my starting point into development.

A Turning Point

A simple real-life incident — someone missing a scholarship due to lack of awareness — changed how I see problems. It made me realize that awareness and access can change lives.

What I'm Doing Now

What Makes Me Different

Future Vision
