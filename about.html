About Me

My journey, experiences, and what shaped me into a developer

Childhood & School

I grew up in Samba, J&K and studied at Kushan Public School. During my school years, I was awarded Student of the Year and represented my district in the SOF Olympiad. These experiences built my confidence and curiosity early on.

Discovering Technology

As an introvert, I found my interest in computers. I spent hours exploring how websites work, how apps take input, and how systems respond. That curiosity led me to learn HTML, which became my first step into the world of development.

A Moment That Changed My Thinking

One evening during a walk, I met a person who told me he had missed a scholarship simply because he didn’t know about it — he didn’t even know what it was or whether he was eligible.

That moment made me realize how lack of awareness can cost people life-changing opportunities.

Life Lesson

My father always told me: "Mistakes happen from everyone, but only a few improve from them."

This mindset has shaped how I approach learning, challenges, and growth.

Engineering Journey

I was selected for the PMSSS scholarship and joined Priyadarshini College of Engineering, Nagpur. I achieved 8.67 SGPA in my first semester and also won 2nd prize in Chemistry in Action.

What I’m Doing Now

Currently, I am focused on building strong fundamentals in C, Python, and Data Structures & Algorithms. I am also working on Scheme Sathi, a project aimed at making government schemes accessible and easy to understand.

Future Vision

My goal is to become a skilled developer who creates meaningful solutions. I want to build products that solve real-world problems and positively impact people’s lives.
