About Me

My journey, experiences, and what drives me forward

Childhood & School

I grew up in Samba, J&K, and completed most of my schooling at Kushan Public School. During this time, I was honored as Student of the Year and also represented my district in the SOF Olympiad.

Discovering Computers

As an introvert, I found comfort and curiosity in computers. I spent hours understanding how websites work and how applications process input. This curiosity led me to learn HTML, marking the beginning of my journey into development.

School Transition

I later joined Adarsh Vidya Peeth, where supportive teachers and a focused academic environment helped strengthen my foundation.

Turning Point

One evening during a walk, I met a person who shared that he had missed a scholarship simply because he didn’t know about it — he didn’t even know if he was eligible or how beneficial it could have been.

That moment stayed with me and made me realize how lack of awareness can cost people life-changing opportunities.

Learning from Life

My father always told me, "Mistakes happen from everyone, but only a few learn and improve from them." This mindset shaped how I approach learning, challenges, and growth.

Engineering Journey

I was selected for the PMSSS scholarship and joined Priyadarshini College of Engineering, Nagpur. In my first semester, I achieved 8.67 SGPA and won 2nd Prize in Chemistry in Action.

Current Focus

I am currently focused on strengthening my skills in C, Python, and Data Structures & Algorithms. I am also building Scheme Sathi, a project aimed at making government schemes more accessible.

Future Vision

My goal is to become a skilled developer who builds impactful solutions and helps people through technology. I believe in continuous growth, learning, and creating value.
