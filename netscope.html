📡 NetScope India

A smart internet performance analysis platform built to remove confusion around network speed, latency, and telecom quality in India.

The Problem

Users constantly face slow internet, buffering, and lag but do not understand the real reason behind it. Network providers show high speeds, but real-world experience is often different.

The Confusion

People cannot easily compare Jio, Airtel, Vi, or BSNL performance. Speed tests only show numbers, not real network quality or stability.

The Idea

Create a simple tool that explains internet performance in a visual and understandable way, combining speed, ping, and signal into one clear system.

The Solution

NetScope India was built to analyze download speed, upload speed, latency, and signal strength, and compare multiple telecom networks in real time.

The Impact

It helps students, gamers, and professionals understand which network works best in their area, improving productivity and reducing frustration caused by poor connectivity.
